    Employee Categories

    Custom labels like CEO, Warehouse, Sales, or Installers — separate from Role (which controls Crew/route access). An employee can belong to more than one category.

    Employee Names, Role & Pay

    Add each employee's name, then set their Role — Crew for install/fix/removal crews (they get the route map), or a custom label like Warehouse or Receptionist. Position is a free-text job title. Choose Hourly or Salary and enter their pay, and assign them to any Categories above. Below each name, pick exactly which parts of their portal they see.

    Manage Crews

    Set how many crews you're running, then assign each employee's default crew. Only officially saved crews show here — one-off changes for a single day belong in One-Day Reassignment instead.

    One-Day Crew Reassignment

    Move someone to a different crew for just one day, without changing their usual (saved) crew.

    Daily Responsibilities

    For Crew employees, this adds an optional extra checklist on top of their route map. For other roles (Warehouse, Receptionist, etc.), this is their whole day — they'll see just this checklist when they log in.
