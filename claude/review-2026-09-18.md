## B. Genuinely still open

### 2 · "Waiting on light colours" should not be there
`whBuildQueueGroups` puts a house in `blocked` only when it is queued to build **and**
`houseLightsText` is empty — i.e. the record genuinely has no colours in either field.
A portal colour change cannot produce that state (the description is written and is what
the warehouse reads), so the houses she is looking at have never had colours recorded.

⛔ **Needs her answer before anything is built** — see question 1. Removing the block
without knowing what should replace it means the warehouse either builds a bundle with
no colours specified, or those houses vanish off the tab entirely, which is the bug
[[WH-27]] was raised to fix.

### 8 · Everything should land in the Inbox
The filters, sub-folders and folder deletion she asks for **already exist** ([[MSG-15]],
[[MSG-20]]): New section, subtabs, drag, right-click, Move to…, and a delete on every
folder she made. What does not match her sentence is the first half — `MESSAGE_HOME_FOLDER`
auto-files **five topics** out of the Inbox before she ever sees them:

    Cancellation Request                     → Cancellations
    Note Added                               → Member Portal
    Existing Customer - Address Changed      → Member Portal
    Member error / Admin error               → the two Errors folders

So "everything goes into Inbox and then I file it" is exactly what the app does not do
today. Her own filing already wins over this table (`filedByHand` is tested first); what
is missing is the *arrival* being the Inbox.

⚠ The two error topics are deliberately doubled up — index.html writes those folder names
directly because it has no login and cannot read this table. Unpicking the three
customer-facing ones is straightforward; the error pair needs care.

### 10 · The RSVP question should go away once answered
