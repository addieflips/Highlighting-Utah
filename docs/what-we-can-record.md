## Why this list exists

Q-007 asked what the real option set was. That is answered: these fourteen,
derived from the code and corrected by Addie on 2026-08-21, with
`options-audit.test.js` holding them in place.

What is **still open**, and can only ever be answered by Addie, is the question
underneath it: **is anything sold today that has no row here at all?**

⚠ **No test can find that one.** Every check in this repo works by comparing a
declared option against the artifacts it has to reach — so an option nobody ever
declared cannot fail anything. It is invisible by construction, permanently, and
the only detector is somebody who knows the business reading the list.

Two things that are **not** open, recorded so they are not asked again:

- **What sets the price.** Only measured feet. Sides raise a re-quote because the
  FOOTAGE changes; they are not a second price input (`houseSides.affectsPrice`).
- **Where each answer must reach.** Frozen in the AGREED map in
  `options-audit.test.js`. That changes when she changes her mind, not otherwise.

## The 14

### Measured feet

How many feet of roofline you are lighting.

- **Asked at:** the quote form
- **Sets the price:** Yes — this is the only thing that does
- **Has to reach:** Quote · Confirmation · Customer record · Warehouse pull list · Invoice

### Light colours

Which colours, and the order if they alternate.

- **Asked at:** the quote form and the member portal

### Wire colour

- **Asked at:** the quote form and the member portal

## How to read it

Go down the list once and ask one thing of each: *is this something we sell, or
something we ask?* Then the harder one: **what do we sell that is not on here?**

Anything missing is not broken — it is absent. Nothing will complain, no test
will fail, and it will simply never appear on a quote, a crew sheet or a pull
list. That is what makes the ten minutes of reading worth it.
