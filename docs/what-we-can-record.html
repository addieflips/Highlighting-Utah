No test can find that. Every check in the system works by comparing an option against the places it has to reach — so an option nobody ever wrote down cannot fail anything. It is invisible by design, permanently, and the only way to catch it is somebody who knows the business reading the list.

Anything missing is not broken. It is absent: nothing complains, and it simply never appears on a quote, a crew sheet or a pull list.
